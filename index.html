Dihedral Angle ψ ≈ 54.49° (≤ 55°) Does Not Exceed

The dihedral angle between plane $ABE$ and plane $ACE$ is $\angle CPD \approx 54.49^\circ$. Since $54.49^\circ \le 55^\circ$, the angle does not exceed $55^\circ$.

Distance AC ≈ 59.11 cm b(i)

1. Since $D$ is the projection of $C$ on plane $ABE$, $CD \perp \text{plane } ABE \implies CD \perp AD$.

2. In flat $\triangle BCD$, $CD_{\text{3D}} = \sqrt{CK^2 - DK^2} \approx 24.16\text{ cm}$.

3. $AD = AB - BD = 66 - 12.05 \approx 53.95\text{ cm}$.

AC = √(53.95² + 24.16²) ≈ 59.11 cm

Part (a) Flat Paper Card (a)

• In $\triangle BCD$, $\angle BDC = 180^\circ - 110^\circ - 20^\circ = 50^\circ$.
By Sine Rule: $BD = \frac{27\sin 20^\circ}{\sin 50^\circ} \approx \mathbf{12.05\text{ cm}}$.

• In $\triangle BCK$, $BE \perp CD \implies \angle KBC = 70^\circ$, so $\angle ABE = 110^\circ - 70^\circ = 40^\circ$.
By Sine Rule in $\triangle ABE$: $AE = \frac{66\sin 40^\circ}{\sin 121.36^\circ} \approx \mathbf{49.68\text{ cm}}$.

Theorem of 3 Perpendiculars Proof

Drop $DP \perp AE$ at $P$. Since $CD \perp \text{plane } ABE$, by the Theorem of Three Perpendiculars, $CP \perp AE$. Therefore $\angle CPD$ is the plane angle between $ABE$ and $ACE$.
$DP = AD\sin 18.64^\circ \approx 17.24\text{ cm}$.
$\tan \angle CPD = \frac{CD}{DP} = \frac{24.16}{17.24} \approx 1.4016 \implies \angle CPD \approx \mathbf{54.49^\circ}$.
